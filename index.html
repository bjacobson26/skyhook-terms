Reporting a problem

Include your device model, iOS version, Skyhook version, and what happened. A screenshot can help. Please leave out passwords, payment details, and other information we do not need.

Starting fresh

On iPhone, delete Skyhook and reinstall it to remove local progress and settings. Offloading the app keeps its data. Device backups can restore an older save. There is no Skyhook account or cloud-save service operated by the developer.

Sound and controls

Use Music and SFX sliders in Settings to adjust the mix. The speaker button mutes all game audio. Tap to launch from an orbit, and use Retry to return to your current checkpoint.
